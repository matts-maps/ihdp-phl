## Finding a layer

Browse the full list on the [homepage](/), or use the **Layers** section in the sidebar. Each card shows the category, geometry type, feature count, and whether the layer is hosted here yet.

## Downloading a layer

Open a layer's page for full metadata: attributes, use cases, licence, and a download section.

Two states, shown at the bottom of each layer page:

- **Hosted for download** - the layer has been uploaded and the page links straight to it.
- **Source link only** - not yet uploaded here; the page links to the original source (HDX, Geofabrik, or USGS) instead.

Most layers are currently source-linked. Data is being uploaded and re-linked incrementally; check the [changelog](/guides/changelog/) for progress.

## Formats

Formats vary by layer and are listed on each layer's page - typically GeoPackage, Shapefile, GeoJSON, CSV, or GeoTIFF depending on the source.

## Requesting a layer or reporting an issue

Contact details are on each layer's page. For anything broader (a missing layer, a data quality issue, a broken link), open an issue on the [GitHub repository](https://github.com/YOUR_GITHUB_USERNAME/humanitarian-data-package-phl/issues).
